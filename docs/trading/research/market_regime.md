## 7. Regime labels

### BTC: **trending** (bearish drift) — moderate conviction
Evidence for: −8.75% over 30d with lower highs since the Oct 4 $86.5k close; loss of the 50d; a net-outflow ETF month ending with its worst day; a Fed that hiked in September, held in October with two hawkish dissents and a hot CPI; stablecoin supply −2% over 30d led by USDC; sentiment 70 → 36.
Evidence against: 90d still ≈ +24%; price above the 200d; funding neutral and OI not building (so the trend is not leveraged); DVOL in the high-30s. This is a low-vol grind lower, not an impulsive trend.
Why not *mean_reverting*: the 30d drift and the macro vector are one-directional and the 200d–50d band ($72.9k–$81.8k) is too wide to trade as a range. Why not *high_vol*: DVOL 36–41 is near 2021–26 lows. Why not *crisis*: funding is neutral, liquidations modest, no stablecoin or venue break.

**Flip conditions.** → *mean_reverting*: daily close back above the 50d (~$81.8k) with funding still < 0.01%, or two weeks holding the 200d with ETF inflows on ≥ 3 of 5 days. → *high_vol*: DVOL > 45 plus two ±5% days in a week, or weekly ETF flow beyond ±$1.5B. → *crisis*: close below the 200d ($72.9k) + funding < −0.01% for three days + stablecoin 30d change worse than −5%, or a major venue/stablecoin incident.

### ETH: **trending** (bearish) — higher conviction
Evidence for: −13% over 30d, −20% from the September high, lost the 200d EMA, a record 23-day ETF outflow streak led by the largest issuer (ETHA), OI −10–12% m/m, an exit-queue spike to 851K ETH (Oct 2, MetaMask validator exits), and Glamsterdam's Hoodi activation postponed (Oct 22) with mainnet now likely 2027.
Evidence against: 36.1% of supply staked (44.1M ETH); entry queue still 1.16M ETH (20-day wait); 90d ≈ +20%.

**Flip conditions.** → *mean_reverting*: five consecutive ETF inflow days plus a reclaim of $2,320 (200d). → *high_vol*: a ±8% day or ETH implied vol > 60 (ETH vol index not sourced). → *crisis*: close below $1,860 (Jul 31 level) with the outflow streak beyond 30 days.
